moveworks_ai Publisher

moveworks_ai Publisher is a private publishing tool operated by the creator of the moveworks_ai channels. It publishes short vertical videos that the creator produces (Korean-language AI and technology news summaries) to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access any other user's account.

How it works

  1. The creator produces a short video from a cited news source.
  2. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes posting to their own account.
  3. The tool shows the creator's TikTok account, a preview of the video, its caption and the privacy options returned by TikTok, and uploads it via the Content Posting API.
