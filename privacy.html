Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Who uses this tool

moveworks_ai Publisher is used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and the creator information returned by the Content Posting API, used only to show which account a video will be posted to and which privacy options are available.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own account.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer and are never shared, sold or sent to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.

Data we do not collect

We do not access other users' accounts, followers, messages, or any personal data of viewers.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Revoking access and deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
